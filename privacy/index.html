Privacy Policy

Mindrop: Flashcards & Study (Android, package io.github.namhoai1210.mindrop)
Effective date: November 1, 2026

EnglishTiếng Việt

This policy explains what information the Mindrop app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Mindrop is built so that your study content stays on your device. We do not run a server that stores your notes, decks or study history.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following data is created by you and stored locally in the app's private storage. We never receive it:

  • Notes, flashcards, decks, tags and study/review history.
  • Photos you take or pick, and audio you record, to attach to a note.
  • Settings, themes, reminders, in-app coins and game progress.
  • Files you import (CSV, TSV, XLSX and similar) or export. These are only read or written when you choose a file.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this permanently. Make an export first if you want to keep a copy.

2. Device permissions

PermissionUsed for
Camera / PhotosAttaching a picture you choose to a note.

Every permission is optional. You can refuse it or revoke it in Android settings; only the related feature stops working.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads on non-study screens and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-app coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, and app instance identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads.

This data is collected and handled by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the app's Settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Optional AI feature

AI is off by default. There are two ways to use it:

  • Your own Gemini API key. If you save a key, the app sends the note fields you select directly from your device to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) over HTTPS, using your key. That content is processed under the terms of your own Google account and the Gemini API Terms. Your key is stored encrypted with Android Keystore, is never included in backups or exports, and is never sent to us. You can delete it in Settings at any time.
  • External AI via file. The app can create a prompt that you copy into any AI service yourself, then import the result as a file. In this case the app sends nothing; what you paste into another service is governed by that service's policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your notes or study content for advertising.

6. Data sharing summary

Data leaves your device only in these cases: ad-related data processed by Google AdMob (section 3), content you choose to send to Gemini with your own key (section 4), and files you choose to export or share.

7. Security

Data in transit to Google uses HTTPS. Local data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what leaves your device to the cases above.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through ads in the app, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, your study data is fully under your control on the device: you can edit, export or delete it in the app. For ad data held by Google, use your Google account and Android ad settings, or the in-app privacy options. You may contact us with any privacy request.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
